I am a robotics engineer at SteerAI in Abu Dhabi, working on ground autonomy and localization. My work focuses on helping robots understand where they are and navigate reliably, through localization, SLAM, mapping, and coverage planning.

Previously, I worked at LionsBot International in Singapore, where I built autonomy software for a fleet of more than 2,500 cleaning robots. I also developed a desktop 2D/3D map editor and a map-data format shared across robots, desktop tools, and cloud systems.

I conducted research at the ROAR Lab, Singapore University of Technology and Design, and earlier at HuT Labs, Amrita University. I hold a dual master’s in Nano Electronic Engineering Design from SUTD and Chang Gung University, and a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Amrita University. I am a co-inventor on two published robotics patent families.

You can reach me at ghanta1996@gmail.com.
